HOW TO PUT THESE FILES LIVE
============================

IMPORTANT: Keep the folders (src, public) intact. Do NOT drag files out of them.

The correct structure must look like this in your repo:

  index.html
  package.json
  package-lock.json
  vite.config.ts
  tsconfig.json
  tsconfig.app.json
  tsconfig.node.json
  README.md
  public/
    favicon.svg
  src/
    App.tsx
    main.tsx
    index.css
    components/
      Hero.tsx
      Nav.tsx
      Projects.tsx
      Experiences.tsx
      About.tsx
      Contact.tsx
      Footer.tsx
      icons/
        LinkedinIcon.tsx

Easiest method: use GitHub Desktop (desktop.github.com) to clone your repo,
delete the old files, copy these in WITH their folders, then Commit + Push.
